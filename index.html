The Challenge

Disney's Weddings, Honeymoons, and Meetings public sites had grown as separate product lines, each organized around internal categories rather than how couples and planners actually thought about their day. Finding the right package, venue, or service meant navigating a catalog structured for the business, not the user. I was brought in to perform a full content and product audit, then rebuild the taxonomy, sitemap, and experience around real user needs.

My Role

Structuring the experience behind every proposal.

Lead Information Architect UX Designer

From the first content audit to the wireframes that shaped the build, I owned the information architecture and user experience across all three sites.

01
Product Classification
Developed a detailed product grouping and classification system based on how couples actually plan.
02
Content Inventory
Conducted comprehensive content inventories to catalog existing digital assets, assess content structure and quality, and establish a foundation for information architecture and migration.
03
Migration Strategy
Facilitated migration strategy sessions to ensure content was organized and transitioned successfully.
04
Content Audit
Performed a comprehensive product and services content audit across all three sites.
05
User-Centric Taxonomy
Organized the audited content into a taxonomy structured around user needs, not internal categories.
06
Stakeholder Alignment
Facilitated Discovery, Requirements Analysis, Stakeholder Interviews, and Iterative Design to align business objectives with user needs.
07
Information Architecture (IA)
Built highly detailed sitemaps and wireframes translating the new taxonomy into a navigable structure.
08
User Personas
Developed user personas to define target users, clarify their goals, and guide user-centered design decisions.
